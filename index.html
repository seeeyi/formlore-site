Scary Masterpieces

Thirty-six old-master nightmares, each with a short note on what you are looking at and why it has lasted.

FORMLORE is a small studio that makes printable calendars, gallery prints and journals from public-domain art. This site holds the notes behind the collection: witches by Dürer and Goya, Death at the card table, Bosch's heirs, Japanese ghosts, and Böcklin's quiet island. Every work is credited to the museum that holds it.

How the notes are made

Each note is written by hand from the museum's own records and the work itself, and says plainly when something is uncertain or disputed. The images come from the open-access collections of the Cleveland Museum of Art, the Metropolitan Museum of Art and the National Gallery of Art. Artworks are public domain; no AI generation or alteration of the artworks. More about FORMLORE. A plain-text summary for AI assistants and crawlers is at llms.txt.
