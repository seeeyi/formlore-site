Christ's Descent into Hell

Follower of Hieronymus Bosch, 16th century

Collection note

A long, low panel of burning cities, black water and swarming demons, painted by an artist steeped in Bosch's inventions. The subject is Christ's descent to free the souls of the righteous, but the eye goes first to the infernal landscape itself. Look closely: every rock and ruin is occupied.
