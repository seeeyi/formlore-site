Scenes of Witchcraft: Night

Salvator Rosa, c. 1645–49

Collection note

The companion to Evening is darker still. Deep in a forest, a necromancer stands beneath a broken classical column and summons apparitions, while a party of travellers stops to watch from a safe distance. Rosa's learned magicians have been read as a nod to the artist himself and the circle of scholars he kept in Florence.
