The Bewitched Groom

Hans Baldung Grien, 1544

Collection note

A stable groom lies sprawled on the floor, foreshortened so steeply that we look straight up the soles of his feet. A horse glances back at him, and a witch with a torch watches from the window. Baldung made the woodcut in 1544, a year before his death, and its meaning is still argued over.
