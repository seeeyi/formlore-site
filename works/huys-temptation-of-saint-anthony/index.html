The Temptation of Saint Anthony

Attributed to Pieter Huys, 16th century

Collection note

Huys was an Antwerp painter who worked squarely in Bosch's wake, and this panel is a catalogue of borrowed nightmares. The saint, hooded at the left beside his traditional pig, is approached by a nude temptress and a companion bearing a dish, while fires burn on the horizon and hybrid creatures crawl, fly and swim through every corner. It is very nearly five feet wide.
