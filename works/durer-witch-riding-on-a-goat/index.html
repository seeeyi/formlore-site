Witch Riding on a Goat

Albrecht Dürer, c. 1500/1501

Collection note

This engraving is barely larger than a playing card, yet it holds a whole sabbath. A witch rides a flying goat backwards, her hair streaming, while putti busy themselves below. Even Dürer's monogram is printed in reverse: the world turned upside down, right down to the signature.
