Witches' Sabbath

Jacques de Gheyn II, late 16th–early 17th century

Collection note

Hags crouch around a steaming cauldron, one reaching towards the fire, while an animal prowls at their feet and a plume of smoke rolls overhead. De Gheyn returned to witchcraft again and again, and this sheet has the speed of an artist thinking with the pen. The brownish paper gives the whole scene the colour of smoke.
