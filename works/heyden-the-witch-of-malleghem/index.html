The Witch of Malleghem

Pieter van der Heyden after Pieter Bruegel the Elder, 1559

Collection note

Bruegel designed it, van der Heyden engraved it and Hieronymus Cock published it in 1559. It is a satire on quackery: a village “witch” cuts the stones of folly from the heads of the gullible while an accomplice crouches inside a giant egg. The joke, five centuries on, still lands.
