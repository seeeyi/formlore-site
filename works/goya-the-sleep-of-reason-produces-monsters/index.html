The Sleep of Reason Produces Monsters

Francisco de Goya, 1799

Collection note

This is plate 43 of Los Caprichos. A man slumps over his desk as owls and bats pour out of the dark behind him, and a lynx-eyed cat keeps watch. The inscription carved into the desk is Goya's whole argument. It is one of the most quoted images in Western art.
