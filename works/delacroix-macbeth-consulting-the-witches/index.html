Macbeth Consulting the Witches

Eugène Delacroix, 1825

Collection note

Macbeth confronts the witches at their boiling cauldron in this early lithograph. Delacroix worked the stone almost black with greasy crayon, then scratched his design out of the darkness with a sharp point, so the figures seem to surface from smoke. Shakespeare never looked so Romantic.
