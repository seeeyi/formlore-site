Satan Starts from the Touch of Ithuriel's Spear

Henry Fuseli, 1776

Collection note

From Milton's Paradise Lost: the angels Ithuriel and Zephon find Satan crouched as a toad beside the sleeping Adam and Eve, and at the touch of Ithuriel's spear he springs back into his true shape. The lion behind recalls an earlier disguise. Fuseli returned to Milton all his life, and this early drawing already crackles.
