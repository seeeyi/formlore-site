The Four Witches

Albrecht Dürer, 1497

Collection note

Four nude women stand in a close circle, trading glances that give nothing away, while a devil wreathed in flame and smoke lurks in the doorway at the left. The globe hanging above them carries the letters O.G.H., read as O Gott hüte: God protect us. Dürer engraved it in 1497, and the picture has kept its secret ever since.
