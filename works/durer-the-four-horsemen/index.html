The Four Horsemen

Albrecht Dürer, 1498

Collection note

From Dürer's Apocalypse of 1498, the series of fifteen woodcuts that made his name across Europe. The riders of Revelation thunder from left to right, trampling everyone in their path, with Death in the foreground on a starved and bony nag. Rarely has a block of wood moved so fast.
