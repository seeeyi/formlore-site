Ghosts & Yōkai · No. 34 of 36

Demons and Monsters

Takai Kōzan, ca. 1870s

Collection note

Takai Kōzan, a wealthy merchant, scholar and patron of Hokusai, turned in later life to painting yōkai. Here a procession of demons, goblins and grinning monsters tumbles down the scroll in loose, quick ink. It is the night parade as a kind of joyful riot.
