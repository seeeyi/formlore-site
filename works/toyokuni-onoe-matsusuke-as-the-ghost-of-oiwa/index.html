Onoe Matsusuke as the Ghost of Oiwa

Utagawa Toyokuni I, 1812

Collection note

The actor Onoe Matsusuke appears as the vengeful ghost of the murdered wife Oiwa, her long hair loose and her body trailing away into a wisp, ringed by flickering ghost-fire. Ghost plays were a summer staple of the kabuki stage, meant to send a chill through the audience. Toyokuni made the print in 1812.
