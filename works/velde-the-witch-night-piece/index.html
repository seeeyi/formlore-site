The Witch (Night Piece)

Jan van de Velde II, 1626

Collection note

A witch kneels inside her magic circle preparing a brew, surrounded by familiars and the emblems of vice. Van de Velde engraved it in the “dark manner”, carving light out of near-total black. It was made for long nights.
