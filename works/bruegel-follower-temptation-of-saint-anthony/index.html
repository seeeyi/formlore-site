The Temptation of Saint Anthony

Follower of Pieter Bruegel the Elder, c. 1550/1575

Collection note

Here the landscape does the talking: a river, a smoking steeple, a giant fish run aground, and creatures riding fish across the sky. Saint Anthony appears twice, once resisting in a rough shelter and once hauled into the air by demons. It rewards a long look.
