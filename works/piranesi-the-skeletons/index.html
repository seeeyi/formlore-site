The Skeletons

Giovanni Battista Piranesi, 1745–50

Collection note

One of the four Grotteschi that Piranesi etched in the late 1740s, before he became famous for prisons and ruins. Skeletons, tumbled fragments and tangled growth dissolve into a landscape of quick, feathery lines. It reads less like a scene than a fever dream about antiquity.
