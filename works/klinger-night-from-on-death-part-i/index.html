Night, from On Death, Part I

Max Klinger, 1889

Collection note

The opening plate of Klinger's On Death, Part I, a cycle of ten etchings that one critic called a modern Dance of Death. A man sits alone on a bench in a walled garden as a moonbeam breaks over the sea, while a lily and a butterfly wait on the path. Nothing happens yet, and that is the dread.
