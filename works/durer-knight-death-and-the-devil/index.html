Knight, Death, and the Devil

Albrecht Dürer, 1513

Collection note

One of Dürer's three master engravings. An armoured rider presses on through a rocky gorge, ignoring a horned devil behind him and Death beside him, crowned with snakes and holding up an hourglass. A skull lies underfoot, his dog keeps pace, and the city on the hill is still a long way off.
