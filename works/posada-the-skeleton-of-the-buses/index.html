The Skeleton of the Buses

José Guadalupe Posada, c. 1900–10

Collection note

Posada's calaveras were cheap broadsheets of image and verse, sold on the streets of Mexico City to readers who often could not read the text. Here a tram unloads its passengers straight into a crowd of skulls while Death beckons them on, a comment on the city's dangerous new electric streetcars. The graveyard, it suggests, is simply the last stop.
