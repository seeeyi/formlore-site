Scenes of Witchcraft: Evening

Salvator Rosa, c. 1645–49

Collection note

At dusk, hags gather around a cauldron while a conjured skeleton hangs overhead, holding an hourglass. A wax effigy hints at love magic, and the round canvas itself echoes the magic circle. Rosa also wrote verse on the subject. Few paintings are more purely Halloween.
