Dance of Death: The Miser

Hans Holbein the Younger, c. 1526

Collection note

Holbein designed the Dance of Death series and the master block-cutter Hans Lützelburger cut it before his death in 1526. The set was finally published in 1538. Here Death comes for the rich man at his counting table, and no lock or ledger can stop him. The block is tiny but the drama isn't.
