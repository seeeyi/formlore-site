The Crossing · No. 36 of 36

Island of the Dead

Arnold Böcklin, 1880

Collection note

A small boat crosses perfectly still water towards a rocky islet crowded with black cypresses. A figure in white stands at the prow. Böcklin painted this, one of five versions of the subject, in 1880, and it was never really a painting about fear. It is about arriving.
