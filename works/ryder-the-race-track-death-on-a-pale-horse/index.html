The Race Track (Death on a Pale Horse)

Albert Pinkham Ryder, c. 1896–1908

Collection note

The painting grew out of a real loss: a friend of Ryder's staked everything on a horse race, lost, and took his own life. Death rides the empty track with his scythe, and a snake lies in the foreground. Ryder reworked the canvas for years and was reluctant to let it go.
