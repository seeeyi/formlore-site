Death and the Miser

Hieronymus Bosch, c. 1485/1490

Collection note

A dying man sits up in bed as Death, shrouded and holding an arrow, slips through the door. A little demon offers him a bag of gold while an angel turns him towards the crucifix in the window. The panel, probably the wing of a lost altarpiece, is a deathbed lesson drawn from the Ars Moriendi, the art of dying, and even now the miser has not quite chosen.
