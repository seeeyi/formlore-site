The Witches

Hans Baldung Grien, 1510

Collection note

A coven gathers around a jar that erupts into a column of vapour, while one of the witches sails overhead on a flying goat. Baldung printed the design in 1510 from two blocks, a grey tone block beneath the black line block, so the bare paper glows like moonlight. It became one of the defining witch images of the German Renaissance.
