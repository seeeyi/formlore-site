The Hell Courtesan

Utagawa Kunisada II, late 1850s

Collection note

Legend tells of Jigoku-dayū, the Hell Courtesan, who wore robes painted with the torments of hell and became a disciple of the Zen monk Ikkyū. Kunisada II painted her on a tall silk scroll, her robes crowded with scenes of hell. Beauty and damnation, worn as a single garment.
