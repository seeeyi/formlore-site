The Temptation of Saint Anthony (second version)

Jacques Callot, 1635

Collection note

Callot etched this second, larger Temptation in 1635, the year of his death, on a plate nearly half a metre wide. A colossal winged demon fills the sky above a swarming theatre of monsters, while the tiny saint holds firm below. Its swarm of invented creatures proved widely influential.
