Demons & Temptations

Six works about the pull of the monstrous: three very different Temptations of Saint Anthony, a hell painted in Bosch's manner, Fuseli's Satan springing out of a toad, and Goya's owls and bats pouring from a sleeper's dream.

They reward a long look. Every corner of these crowded scenes is occupied.

  • The Temptation of Saint AnthonyFollower of Pieter Bruegel the Elder, c. 1550/1575
  • The Temptation of Saint AnthonyAttributed to Pieter Huys, 16th century
  • Christ's Descent into HellFollower of Hieronymus Bosch, 16th century
  • The Temptation of Saint Anthony (second version)Jacques Callot, 1635
  • Satan Starts from the Touch of Ithuriel's SpearHenry Fuseli, 1776
  • The Sleep of Reason Produces MonstersFrancisco de Goya, 1799
