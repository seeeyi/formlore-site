About

About FORMLORE

FORMLORE makes things for people who like old pictures that still have teeth.

It is a small studio built around one habit: look closely at a work of art, find out what is really going on in it, and make something good enough to live with. The first collection is Scary Masterpieces, thirty-six works of witches, death, demons and ghosts that were made between about 1485 and 1880.

What is on this site

A page for every work, with the artist, date, medium and museum, a link to the museum's own page, and a short note in plain language. The notes are meant to be read in a minute and remembered for longer. Start with Witches, Death, Demons & Temptations, Ghosts & Yōkai or The Crossing, or see all 36 works.

Where the images come from

The images are scans from museums that publish their public-domain holdings openly: the Cleveland Museum of Art (CC0), the Metropolitan Museum of Art (Open Access, CC0) and the National Gallery of Art (Open Access). Nineteen works come from Cleveland, ten from the Met and seven from the National Gallery. The images on this site are reduced for the web. Artworks are public domain; no AI generation or alteration of the artworks.

Questions

What is Scary Masterpieces?

Scary Masterpieces is a collection of thirty-six old-master works about witches, death, demons, ghosts and yōkai, from Dürer and Bosch to Goya, Redon and Böcklin. FORMLORE turned it into a printable 2027 wall calendar, a set of 36 printable gallery prints and a printable journal.

Are the artworks public domain?

Yes. Artworks are public domain; no AI generation or alteration of the artworks. The scans come from the open-access programmes of the Cleveland Museum of Art (CC0), the Metropolitan Museum of Art (Open Access, CC0) and the National Gallery of Art (Open Access). Each work page names its museum and links to the museum's own page.

Who writes the notes?

FORMLORE writes them. Each note is original, based on the museum's catalogue information and a close look at the work, and it is not copied or adapted from any other site or app.

What does FORMLORE sell?

A printable 2027 wall calendar, a pack of 36 printable gallery prints and a printable journal, all as instant digital downloads on Etsy.
