Witches

For about three centuries artists kept returning to the witch. Sometimes she was a warning, sometimes a satire, sometimes simply an excuse to draw the night. The twelve works here run from Dürer's goat-riding witch of around 1500 to Delacroix's Macbeth at the cauldron in 1825.

Look for the small things: a monogram printed backwards, a wax effigy, a woodcut made from two blocks so the paper glows like moonlight. Each note says what is actually going on.

  • The Four WitchesAlbrecht Dürer, 1497
  • The WitchesHans Baldung Grien, 1510
  • The Bewitched GroomHans Baldung Grien, 1544

  • The Witch (Night Piece)Jan van de Velde II, 1626

  • Witches Preparing to FlyFrancisco de Goya, 1796/1797
  • The Night-Hag Visiting Lapland WitchesHenry Fuseli, 1796
  • Macbeth Consulting the WitchesEugène Delacroix, 1825
